    Maternity Nutrition & Wellness Guide

    Eating well for you and your baby

    General guidance from our maternity team. Your doctor will tailor it to your health and reports.

    Weeks 1–12

    First trimester

    • 🥬 Folic-acid rich greens: palak, methi, beans
    • 🍚 Small, frequent meals to ease nausea
    • 🥥 Coconut water, buttermilk & plenty of fluids
    • 🚫 Avoid raw papaya, pineapple & unpasteurised milk

    Weeks 13–27

    Second trimester

    • 🥛 Calcium: milk, curd, paneer, ragi
    • 🫘 Protein: dal, eggs, chana, sprouts
    • 🍎 Iron with vitamin C: dates, jaggery, amla, citrus
    • 🚶‍♀️ Gentle daily walks, as advised

    Weeks 28–40

    Third trimester

    • 🌾 Fibre-rich whole grains & fruits for digestion
    • 🐟 Omega-3: walnuts, flax seeds, well-cooked fish
    • 🧂 Limit salt, sugar & fried snacks
    • 💧 8–10 glasses of water every day
